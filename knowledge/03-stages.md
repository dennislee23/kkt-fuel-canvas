# The bottom-up path and the Red Petroleum reference

## The path runs bottom-up

Every block of the network economy climbs the same way — start small on
existing data, build the foundation as you go. You do not need to be at the top
to begin.

- **Essential — see & control, now.** Bring the picture together on data you
  already have: need, stock, supplier offers, your price against the market.
  Cheap, fast, no models.
- **Advanced — forecast & optimize.** Forecast demand, find the right volume
  and moment, price by local sensitivity. Builds on the foundation beneath it.
- **Frontier — decisions under AI.** Agents prepare the order or hold price
  sharp within your rules — the person sets the bounds and approves. Never AI
  on its own.

## Red Petroleum — the reference case

A leader in Central Asian fuel retail (~$600M revenue, 220+ stations, 13
depots, ~2,500 employees, ~500K customers). The data was there — ERP, POS,
loyalty, logistics — but the network ran on people, calls and manual
reconciliation. KKT delivered one connected program across four fronts:

1. **Supply chain — Optimus, in pilot.** Fuel-order optimisation, real-time
   rail tracking, TMS-integrated dispatch. Early pilot signal: fewer stockouts
   and surpluses, less railcar idle time.
2. **Customer value.** Segmentation across ~300M transactions / ~500K
   customers; a non-fuel recommendation engine in testing. LTV / retention /
   check uplifts are 2026 targets, not realised results.
3. **Operational excellence.** Process re-engineering, AI assistants for HR and
   station operators, OCR, an internal data team trained for handover.
4. **Data foundation.** A central DWH on 500M+ rows; 20+ interactive BI
   reports; a text-to-SQL assistant.

Be precise: the broad program is in production; the **Optimus engine
specifically is a pilot**, not full production. 2026 uplift figures are targets.

## How an engagement runs

Every engagement starts with a two-week diagnostic — scope only, decision-grade
readout. If the case for AI is not there, KKT says so. After the diagnostic, a
sequenced engagement with a senior internal owner who inherits the system.
