# The fuel network economy — the A–G blocks

The fuel offering is the whole network economy, not a single product. KKT helps
across seven connected fronts. Optimus is A+B, one embodiment.

A. **Fuel: procurement, price & margin.** Procurement and price run as one
   commercial loop — forecast the need, choose supplier and moment, account for
   the full landed cost of a batch, set price-at-pump by market and demand.
   Focus: fewer stockouts and surpluses, less overpay, better margin, faster
   commercial calls. (This plus B is Optimus.)

B. **Logistics & fuel supply.** Supply made manageable end-to-end — batch
   movement, delay risk, acceptance discrepancies, railcars and demurrage,
   depot operations, station delivery, trip execution. Focus: fewer losses,
   less idle time and fines, less manual dispatch.

C. **Stations, stores & cafés.** The station run as a profit point, not only an
   operational object — underperformance, queues and lost traffic, dispenser
   and POS violations, shift work, equipment, assortment, write-offs, store
   conversion. Focus: fewer operational losses, higher station revenue, better
   customer experience.

D. **Customers, loyalty & B2B.** The customer base and B2B portfolio turned
   into a managed source of growth — customer value, churn, frequency and
   check, promo, reviews, retail media, corporate-client profitability. Focus:
   higher retention, more margin per customer, fewer wasted discounts, a
   stronger loyalty program.

E. **Finance, money & control.** Finance as a live picture — margin, costs,
   cash, receivables, payment risks, financial losses and policy violations
   become visible before they become a problem. Focus: faster management
   decisions, fewer leaks, better cash control, trust in the numbers.

F. **Supporting functions.** HR, legal, MTO procurement, contractors, internal
   control, documents, back-office. Focus: less manual work, faster processes,
   clearer spend, violations visible before the audit, not after.

G. **Data & processes — the foundation.** One data base, quality and
   availability, reporting, knowledge search, office AI tools, process
   re-engineering and agentic solutions. Focus: one version of truth, less
   manual work, and AI that scales systemically, not point by point.
