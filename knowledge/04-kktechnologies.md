# Kitty Kat Technologies (KKT)

A senior data & AI delivery team with roots in business, based in Tallinn,
Estonia (EU entity). KKT works with mid-sized companies — deepest in retail and
fuel — typically $50M–$500M in revenue, across Central Europe, the EU and
Central Asia.

## What KKT does

The full range, shipped into real operations: data foundations (DWH, BI,
text-to-SQL), ML models (segmentation, demand and price forecasting, dynamic
pricing, optimisation, anomaly detection, recommendation engines), AI
assistants and automation (LLM assistants, OCR, computer vision, agents),
low-risk prototyping ("test before invest"), AI strategy and enablement, and
AI ethics & regulatory.

## How KKT is different

Starts from the business question, not the technology. Strong AI capability is
the baseline; PMO discipline — scope, sequencing, ownership, shipping under
constraint — is what separates a slide-led project from one that runs in
production. Engagements are designed to end: the internal team inherits the
system, not a dependency.
